# Animated Bird, Pigeon

RuneSim adaptations: extracted texture, baked deformation rig and five animation
clips, normalized export axes, Unreal material and population profile. Original
source is preserved. This is a low polygon bird for distant flocks, not a photoreal
close-up asset. Source animation and dimensions are artistic approximations.
